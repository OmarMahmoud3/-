## Brand & Style

This design system channels the energetic, hearty, and authentic atmosphere of traditional Syrian street food and late-night shawarma culture. Drawing visual inspiration directly from handcrafted street chalkboard menus, glowing vertical spits, sizzling charcoal, and iconic folk motifs (such as the classic tarboosh and playful handlebar mustache character mark), the aesthetic pairs an unapologetic midnight charcoal canvas with energetic, appetizing ember-orange accents.

The design movement combines **High-Contrast Street Editorial** with **Tactile Chalkboard Textures**. It captures the spirit of urban culinary craft through:
- Deep, soot-black matte backgrounds that allow culinary photography and hot spiced color highlights to ignite.
- Textured, gestural brush strokes and badge tags that recall hand-painted market signboards and spice rub chalkings.
- High-readability Arabic and Latin typographic hierarchy suited for rapid ordering, dynamic menu browsing, and appetizing combos.
- Punchy, street-level warmth designed to evoke immediate cravings, generosity, and neighborhood authenticity.

## Colors

The palette is engineered around dark-mode indulgence, referencing glowing spit embers, seasoned sumac, garlic toum cream, and dark cast-iron griddles.

- **Primary (`#FF7A00`) — Spiced Searing Orange**: The focal color representing roasting flames, Aleppo paprika seasoning, and energy. Used for call-to-actions, category banners, price highlights, and active tab indicators.
- **Secondary (`#C92A2A`) — Tarboosh Crimson**: Rooted in the traditional crimson felt fez from the brand's mascot, this color signifies urgency, hot spice tiers (spicy toum, dynamite seasoning), and promotional badges.
- **Tertiary (`#F59F00`) — Golden Toasted Crisp**: Echoes golden broasted chicken skin, toasted flatbread ridges, and saffron-cardamom rice fattah. Used for star ratings, combo flags, and secondary pricing tags.
- **Neutral Palette (`#0B0B0B` to `#242424`)**:
  - Deep Background: `#0B0B0B` (pitch chalkboard base).
  - Surface Card: `#161616` (slate charcoal container).
  - Elevated Container / Border: `#282828` (chalk hairline dividers).
  - Text Primary: `#FFFFFF` (crisp chalk white, 100% legibility).
  - Text Muted: `#A0A0A0` (creamy toum ash for descriptive weights, sides, and ingredients).

## Typography

The typographic strategy pairs the geometric impact of **Outfit** for numbers, prices, and Latin branding headers with the authentic Middle Eastern voice of **Cairo** for all Arabic content, item names, and ingredient descriptions.

- **Numerals & Pricing**: Prices are rendered using `Outfit` with high weights (700-900) to replicate the bold white and yellow chalk numbers found on street sandwich menu boards.
- **Arabic Script**: Headings utilize heavy weights (700-800) of Cairo with generous line spacing to prevent diacritics and character ligatures from colliding on mobile displays.
- **RTL Considerations**: Typography naturally respects Right-To-Left layout hierarchies, placing food dish titles on the right and multi-option pricing matrices (Single, Medium, Combo) clearly aligned in tabular columns.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout model is built on an organized, punchy modular grid reflecting compact food boards and delivery menus:
- **Mobile (< 768px)**: 4-column fluid layout with `1rem` margins and `1rem` gutters. Food categories are presented via horizontal swipe carousels and stacked 1-column product cards for fast thumbs-first navigation.
- **Tablet (768px - 1024px)**: 8-column layout with 2-column food grid items and multi-tier pricing tables mirroring physical diner wall menus.
- **Desktop (> 1024px)**: 12-column layout capped at 1280px max width with sticky category indexing on the right sidebar and central visual card feeds.

Grid items use `space-md` (`1rem`) internally to guarantee tactile touch targets of at least 48px for all quick-add and portion selector interactions.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy does not rely on synthetic drop shadows; it relies on **charcoal surface contrast** paired with **subtle fiery radiant glows**:

- **Ground Level (Canvas)**: `#0B0B0B` representing the blackboard slate background.
- **Level 1 (Card & Category Blocks)**: `#161616` background framed with a crisp low-contrast hairline stroke (`1px solid #282828`).
- **Level 2 (Active Selections & Modals)**: `#202020` elevated layer featuring an ambient fiery halo: `box-shadow: 0 8px 24px rgba(255, 122, 0, 0.15)`.
- **Level 3 (Sticky Action Bars & Cart Bottom Sheets)**: `#181818` with a frosted backdrop filter (`backdrop-filter: blur(12px)`) and a vibrant top highlight strip in `#FF7A00`.

## Shapes

The interface balances sturdy urban structure with welcoming comfort. 

- **Containers & Cards**: Base corner radius set to `0.5rem` (`roundedness: 2`), transitioning to `1rem` for larger modal sheets and featured platter displays.
- **Category Headers & Banners**: Emulates brush-stroke badges using slightly asymmetric or pill capsules with tapered, torn, or high-contrast background highlights behind menu headers (`سندوتشات الشاورما`, `الفراخ البروستد`).
- **Quick-Add Buttons**: Circular pill buttons with bold icons allowing fast one-tap ordering.

## Components

### Category Brush Banners
- **Styling**: Distinctive banner elements rendered with an orange solid background (`#FF7A00`) featuring an organic jagged or rounded-pill outline.
- **Typography**: `headline-md` in bold white or black, centered to simulate authentic hand-painted street boards.

### Menu Item Card (The Shawarma Card)
- **Structure**: Surface `#161616` with a hairline `#282828` border. Dish photography positioned with an energetic badge (e.g., "أكثر طلباً" or "سبايسي").
- **Content**: Title (`title-menu-arabic`), item accompaniment line in muted ash (`body-sm`), and a multi-variant pricing matrix (Meat vs. Chicken / Single vs. Combo).
- **CTA**: Plus icon button coated in primary fiery orange with high-contrast black glyph.

### Protein & Size Switchers (Segmented Controls)
- **Structure**: Dark slate capsule containing toggleable tabs (e.g., `فراخ` / `لحمة` / `ميكس`).
- **Active State**: Primary orange background `#FF7A00`, bold white text, instant tactile feedback.
- **Inactive State**: Transparent background, `#A0A0A0` text.

### Buttons & Interactive CTAs
- **Primary Order Button**: Solid `#FF7A00` with `#000000` or `#FFFFFF` text, `0.75rem` vertical padding, bold weight.
- **Spicy / Special Offer Button**: Deep Crimson `#C92A2A` with `#FFFFFF` text.
- **Secondary / Addons Button**: Ghost button with `1.5px solid #FF7A00` and orange text.

### Sticky Order Bar & WhatsApp Quick Dial
- Fixed bottom mobile container displaying live cart item count, total price in `Outfit` numerals, and an immediate checkout action styled in glowing fiery orange with telephone/WhatsApp direct dispatch.
